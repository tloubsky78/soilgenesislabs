A Future Where Technology Serves Life

Food sovereignty connects the health of land with the health and independence of people. It is a major initiative within our broader work to restore the conditions that allow living systems to flourish.

We work toward a future in which:

  • People grow healthy food close to home

  • Growers build lasting relationships with their land

  • Communities gain greater food sovereignty

Restoring the relationship between people, land and life.

It Began as Listening

Scientific inquiry, creative experience and reciprocal care

We did not set out to build a product. We set out to listen.

We regard microbes, fungi and plants as sentient participants in the living systems we learn from. Their responses, relationships and functions contribute to our work. Our reciprocal commitment is to restore supporting conditions, reduce harm and attend to how they respond.

Our work begins with attention:

Living Relationships

Fungi, microbes, roots, water and minerals form changing relationships. We study the system through those relationships.

Sound and Image

The Soil Signal Explorer translates measured living-soil signals into sound and image. Changes become perceptible alongside the data, offering another way to experience living systems.

Time and Change

Continuous observation follows changes that a single visit or sample may miss. Sound and image are translations of those measurements.

Read. Interpret. Restore. Verify.

Our development follows a living loop. Each observation informs the work; each intervention becomes an opportunity to learn.

01 Read

Record changing conditions in living soil over time.

02 Interpret

Examine patterns alongside the conditions and independent measurements that give them meaning.

03 Restore

Develop ways to support soil structure, biology and vegetation.

04 Verify

Compare what changed with the starting conditions and relevant references.

05 Read Again

Let the response inform the next decision.

06 Keep the Evidence

Keep traceable measurements, thoughtful comparisons and clear accounts of what the work establishes.

Built Around the Living System Itself.

Sensing, interpretation and restoration development work together, from the soil beneath a garden to questions spanning landscapes.

Continuous Observation

The Soil Signal Explorer (SSE) records changes in living soil's bioelectrochemical conditions. It adds continuous temporal context between laboratory, biological and ecological measurements. For carbon applications, authoritative carbon measurements remain the anchor.

Restoration Applications

Our research explores how to restore supporting conditions for soil, roots and fungi. Biological products and delivery tools are pathways within this wider development work.

Land and Landscape

We are developing applications for growers, restoration programs and landscape portfolios, connecting local observations with the questions that guide land stewardship.
